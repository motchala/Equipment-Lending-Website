# Equipment Borrowing: A Simple Guide

*PUPSync · PUP Biñan Campus*

This guide explains how borrowing equipment works, in plain words. The numbers below are the usual ones. A Super Admin can change them in Settings, so your school's numbers may be slightly different.

---

## 1. The basics

- **Faculty** can borrow equipment for themselves, or for a student to pick up.
- **Students** do not need an account. A student borrows by using a **code** given by a faculty member.
- **Admins** approve requests and hand out and receive the equipment.
- **Super Admins** do everything an Admin does, and they also change the rules and manage accounts.

---

## 2. The rules at a glance

| What | The usual rule |
|---|---|
| Borrowing hours | 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Everything must be returned by 8:00 PM. |
| Borrowing days | Monday to Saturday. **No borrowing on Sundays.** |
| How far ahead | Up to 14 days from today. |
| How long (hours) | 1, 2, 3 or 6 hours. |
| How long (days) | **Faculty only:** 1 to 3 days, if your school allows it. Students borrow by the hour only. |
| Start time | On the hour (for example 9:00, 10:00). |
| Different items a faculty member can have at once | 5 |
| Students a faculty member can help in one day | 3 |
| Faculty items and students combined, in one day | 7 |
| Different items per student request | 2 |
| Extending a loan | Once per loan |
| Cancelling an approved later-date booking | Up to 1 hour before it starts |

**How the "7" works:** each different item you borrow counts as 1, and each student you help counts as 1. Borrowing 2 of the same item still counts as 1.

---

## 3. How to borrow

### If you are Faculty

1. Open **Borrow** and choose your equipment. You can add several items to one request.
2. Choose where it will be used (a room, or "Outside").
3. Choose the date, the start time and how long you need it.
4. Choose who will pick it up: **you**, or **a student**. If a student, type the student's name (up to 50 letters).
5. Submit your request.

If the request is for a later date, you can attach a request letter (JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 5 MB). Some schools make the letter compulsory.

### If you are a Student

1. Ask a faculty member for a **borrowing code**. Each code works **once**.
2. Open the **Student Portal**. Enter the code, your name (up to 50 letters) and your course.
3. Choose your items (up to 2), where you will use them, the date, the start time and the number of hours.
4. Submit your request.

You **cannot** borrow if the faculty member who gave you the code has an item that is overdue.

---

## 4. What happens after you submit

### Same-day requests: the 10-minute review window

1. The system checks your request right away. If it cannot work (for example, the item is not available, or you reached a limit), you are told **immediately** and nothing is saved.
2. If it looks fine, your request waits for **10 minutes**.
3. During those 10 minutes, an admin can approve or decline it.
4. If no admin acts, the system decides by itself when the 10 minutes are over:
   - **Approved**, if the item is free for that time.
   - **Declined**, if something got in the way. You will see the reason.

If a request has several items, they are **all approved or all declined together**.

### Later-date requests

- An admin must approve them. They are **never approved automatically**.
- If nobody decides, the request is **declined automatically at 12:01 AM on the day you wanted the equipment**. That way you find out with the whole day left to plan.

---

## 5. Picking up and returning

1. When your request is approved, you get a **Claim QR code** in **My Activity**.
2. Show it to an admin at the Admin Office. They scan it, and the item is now **On Loan**.
3. When you bring the item back, show your **Return QR code**. An admin scans it, and you are done.

If an approved item is **never picked up** and the time passes, the request is cancelled automatically.

---

## 6. Changing your mind

### Cancelling (faculty)

| Your request is... | Can you cancel? |
|---|---|
| Waiting for approval | Yes, any time |
| Approved for **today**, not picked up yet | Yes, any time |
| Approved for a **later date** | Yes, until 1 hour before it starts |
| Already picked up, returned or declined | No |

### Extending (faculty)

- You can extend **one time** per loan.
- You can ask when the item is **due in 15 minutes or less**, or when it is already **overdue**.
- You can add **1, 2, 3 or 5 hours** (or **1 day** for a day loan).
- It only works if nobody else has booked the item for that extra time.

---

## 7. If something is overdue

An item is **Overdue** when it is not returned by its return time.

- An admin can send the faculty member a reminder.
- Students **cannot** use a faculty member's code while that faculty member has an overdue item.
- If your school turns on the "overdue block", that faculty member also cannot borrow again until the item is returned.

---

## 8. What admins do

**Every Admin can:**
- Approve or decline requests. **Declining needs a reason** (at least 5 letters). Approving does not, but the system will not let you approve a request that would overbook the item.
- Scan the Claim and Return QR codes.
- Hand out or receive equipment by hand, and handle partial handovers and returns.
- Send overdue reminders.
- Add and edit equipment in the list.

**Only a Super Admin can:**
- Change the rules and limits in Settings.
- Create and manage faculty and admin accounts.
- Sync the equipment list with the connected spreadsheet.
- Delete all loan records (this asks for their password first).

---

## 9. Quick answers

**Why was my request declined?**
The usual reasons: not enough of that item for your time, someone else already booked it, you reached a limit, there is an overdue item (if your school uses that rule), or a letter was missing (if your school requires one). The reason is shown with the request.

**Can a student borrow equipment for days?**
No. Students borrow by the hour only.

**Can I borrow on a Sunday?**
No. Sundays are closed.

**What if nobody approves my later-date request?**
It is declined automatically at 12:01 AM on the day you wanted the equipment.

**Do I need to keep the page open for the 10-minute wait?**
No. You can leave. Look at **My Activity** later to see the result.
