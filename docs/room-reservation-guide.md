# Room Reservation: A Simple Guide

*PUPSync · PUP Biñan Campus*

This guide explains how reserving a room works, in plain words. The numbers below are the usual ones. A Super Admin can change them in Settings, so your school's numbers may be slightly different.

---

## 1. The basics

- **Faculty** can reserve rooms.
- **Students** do not need an account. A student asks for a room by using a **code** given by a faculty member.
- **Admins** review and manage reservations and rooms.
- **Super Admins** do everything an Admin does, and they also change the rules, add the class schedule and manage accounts.

---

## 2. The rules at a glance

| What | The usual rule |
|---|---|
| Reservation hours | 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM |
| Reservation days | Monday to Saturday. **No reservations on Sundays.** |
| How far ahead | Up to 3 weeks from today |
| Longest single reservation | 13 hours |
| Active reservations per faculty | 2 at a time |
| Cancelling an approved reservation | Up to 1 hour before it starts |
| Cancelling a waiting reservation | Any time |

A reservation is **active** while it is Waiting or Approved and has not finished yet. Reservations that students make with your code count toward your 2.

Having equipment out or overdue **never** stops you from reserving a room.

---

## 3. How to reserve a room (faculty)

1. Open **Facilities** and choose a room.
2. Choose the date, the start time and the end time.
3. Add your purpose. For a later date you can attach a request letter (JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 5 MB). Some schools make the letter compulsory.
4. Submit.

**Your request is turned down right away if:**
- the time is already in the past, or the end is before the start;
- the room is under **Maintenance** or **Not Bookable**;
- the day is closed (Sunday) or the time is outside the open hours;
- it is longer than 13 hours, or too far ahead;
- you already have 2 active reservations;
- a **class** is assigned to that room at that time;
- **someone else** has reserved or requested the same room at an overlapping time, even by a few minutes. (Back-to-back is fine: one can end at 10:00 and the next can start at 10:00.)

If your request is turned down, you are told why **immediately** and nothing is saved.

---

## 4. What happens after you submit

### Same-day reservations: the 10-minute review window

1. If it passes the checks, your request waits **10 minutes**. (If the start time is closer than 10 minutes, it waits only until the start time.)
2. During that time, an admin can **approve or decline it immediately**.
3. If no admin acts, the system decides by itself when the time is up:
   - **Approved**, if the room is still free, with no class and no overlap.
   - **Declined**, if something got in the way. You will see the reason.
4. You will see a **countdown** on your screen and in **My Activity**.

**First come, first served.** The first request keeps the time. Nobody can take over a slot by being more important.

### Later-date reservations

- An admin must approve them. They are **never approved automatically**.
- If nobody decides, the request is **declined automatically at 12:01 AM on the day of the reservation**, so you can plan something else.

---

## 5. Students

1. Ask a faculty member for a **code**. Each code works **once**.
2. Open the **Student Portal**. Enter the code, your name (up to 50 letters) and your course.
3. Choose the room, the date and the time.

The same hours, days and limits apply. A few things are different for students:
- Every student request waits for an **admin to decide**. There is no 10-minute automatic approval.
- You **cannot** reserve if the faculty member who gave you the code has an overdue equipment item.
- Your request counts toward that faculty member's 2 active reservations. If they already have 2, they must cancel one first.

---

## 6. Other things faculty can do

- **Cancel:** a waiting reservation any time, an approved one until 1 hour before it starts. Anyone on the waitlist for that slot gets an email when it opens up.
- **Join a waitlist:** if the slot you want is taken, you can join the waitlist. You will be emailed if it becomes free. Joining does not hold the slot for you.
- **Report a problem:** tell the admins if something is wrong with a room. This does not close the room by itself. An admin looks at it first.
- **Ask the room assistant:** a chat helper that answers questions about rooms and your reservations. It can also help you start a reservation, with the same rules as the normal form. You can send up to 20 messages per hour.

---

## 7. Classes

Super Admins add the class schedule. If a class is using a room at a certain time, that room **cannot be reserved** for that time. A class is never moved or cancelled to make room for a reservation.

---

## 8. What admins do

**Every Admin can:**
- Approve or decline waiting reservations. **Declining needs a reason** (at least 5 letters). Approving does not. The system will refuse an approval if a class, an overlap or a closed room makes it impossible.
- Change (reschedule) or cancel a reservation.
- Add and edit rooms, unless a room is locked.
- Read and resolve reported room problems.

**Only a Super Admin can:**
- Change the rules and limits in Settings.
- Add the class schedule.
- Lock or unlock a room, archive a room, or permanently delete an archived room.
- Delete all reservations.

---

## 9. Quick answers

**Why was my reservation declined?**
The usual reasons: the room is taken or has a class, you already have 2 active reservations, the time is outside the open hours, or nobody approved a later-date request in time. The reason is shown with the request.

**Can I reserve a room on a Sunday?**
No. Sundays are closed.

**Will my same-day reservation be confirmed right away?**
Not instantly. It waits up to 10 minutes. An admin can approve it sooner.

**What if nobody approves my later-date reservation?**
It is declined automatically at 12:01 AM on the day of the reservation.

**Do I have to keep the page open?**
No. Check **My Activity** later to see the result.
